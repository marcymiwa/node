About Marcy

I'm Marcy (Mayuko Miwa),
a creative technologist.

I make interactive works that change with how people behave around them.

I tie together visuals, sound and light with TouchDesigner and Blender.

Rooms and works

The Garden

The garden at the heart of the greenhouse, with a spiral staircase you can climb.

  • To Make Is to Live. — 2026 / Tokyo / Essay
    An essay rebuilt as a 3D spiral staircase, read one step at a time as you climb.

1. Kaichiku Bus Stop

A room that recreates the original exhibition space. You can see and touch the installations.

  • Final Adornment — 2026 / Kaichiku / Installation
    An installation where you can play by putting lipstick on 3D data.
  • Last Moisture — 2026 / Kaichiku / Installation
  • Kakawaru (To Relate) — 2026 / Kaichiku / Installation
  • Why My Energy Is So High — 2026 / Kaichiku / Essay installation
    An essay turned into an installation, recreating the day it was shown.

2. Solo Exhibition CURA

The room of my solo show, home of my main piece. Press and hold to touch it.

  • You touch me, and I mirror you. — 2026 / Tokyo / Installation
    Touch it, and light and sound begin to flow. The work mirrors the hand that touches it.

3. blankspace

A place for prototypes — things I made to try ideas out.

  • The “Good” Game (working title) — 2026 / TouchDesigner (.tox)

4. VJ Booth

Things I used for VJ sets in Shibuya. Mix visuals on the deck — get groovy!

  • Playful Classics — 2026 / Tokyo / Video
    Visuals that move with live classical music.

5. Shop

A little shop of electronics, physical works and NFT art.

  • The Chocolate One (working title) — 2026 / Website
  • Lighting Work (working title) — 2026 / Object
